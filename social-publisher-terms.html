O.F. Social Publisher Terms of Service

Effective: September 27, 2026

O.F. Social Publisher is a desktop workflow for authorized Oculus Fontis creators to prepare and post their own short videos to connected social media accounts. These terms apply to that workflow only.

Authorized use

Use the workflow only with an account you are authorized to manage and with content you have the right to publish. Review the video, caption, audience settings, and required disclosures before posting. A command to publish can create a post on the selected platform.

Platform rules

Use of TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube remains subject to each platform's terms, policies, permissions, and technical limits. Platform review or authorization may restrict whether a post can be public. Oculus Fontis does not guarantee that a platform will accept, publish, or retain a submission.

Access and removal

The account owner may disconnect access in the platform's settings and remove local credentials. Removing local credentials does not automatically remove posts already published; manage those through the relevant platform.

START WITH REST

從睡眠與放鬆出發，
慢慢找回生活的餘裕。

Begin with rest.
Make space for life.

Oculus Fontis 希望陪伴每個人找回平靜，照顧身心，建立充實而豐盛的生活。我們以睡眠與放鬆為創作方向，作品會隨著創作步調陸續加入。

Oculus Fontis is about caring for yourself and making room for a calm, fulfilling life. Our creative direction begins with sleep and relaxation. New works will be added as they are ready.
